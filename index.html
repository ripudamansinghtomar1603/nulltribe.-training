ISO 27001 · Practice

Risk Assessment Workbook

Work one risk at a time, the way a real ISO 27001 assessment does: name it, score it, decide what to do about it. Every finished risk joins your register — export to Excel whenever you're ready.

1

Choose a scenario

Every scenario below comes from things that genuinely go wrong in small and mid-size organizations. Pick one that feels close to home.

2

Name the threat and the weakness

A threat is what could cause harm. A vulnerability is the weakness that lets it. It takes both to make a risk.

3

Rate impact and likelihood

One scale for everything: 1, 2 or 3. Rate the impact on each of the three things security protects — confidentiality, integrity, availability — then rate how likely the whole thing is.

Value
Impact means…
Likelihood means…
1 · Low
Minor harm. Little cost or disruption — absorbed in normal work.
Unlikely. No history; would need unusual conditions.
2 · Medium
Noticeable harm. Real cost or downtime — recoverable with effort.
Possible. Happens in the industry; could happen to you this year.
3 · High
Serious harm. Major financial, legal or reputational damage.
Expected. Happens often, or conditions strongly favour it.
ConfidentialityWould the wrong people see the information?
IntegrityCould the information be altered or corrupted?
AvailabilityWould systems or data be down or lost?
LikelihoodHow likely is this within a year?
4

Review the raw risk

Raw risk is your exposure before you change anything: impact × likelihood, where impact is the highest of your C, I and A ratings.

Rate impact and likelihood above to see the predicted raw risk.
5

Decide the treatment

Four legitimate options. The only wrong move is not choosing.

6

Act on your decision

What this step asks for depends on the treatment you chose.

Choose a treatment in step 5 — this step will adapt to your decision.
